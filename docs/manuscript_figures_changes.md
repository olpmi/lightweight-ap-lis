## How the cytology material counts were reconciled

The three panels disagreed because the fixture invented a material hierarchy
instead of using the one the application builds. It registered two specimens (one
with smears, one with a cell block) and then hand-created two blocks on the first
and one on the second, which is why accessioning said "2 smears, 0 cell blocks"
while the material panel showed two blocks and three H&E slides.

The preparation is now declared once, in `FIGURE_PREPARATION`, and every panel
derives from it: **2 smears, 0 ThinPrep, 1 cell block** on **one** pleural-fluid
specimen. The fixture now reproduces `OrderEntryPage`'s own logic rather than
inventing material:

| What the application does | Result in the figures |
| --- | --- |
| Direct preparations (smears, ThinPrep) go onto a single carrier block as typed slides | `CN-26-111-A1` → `A1-S1 [Smear]`, `A1-S2 [Smear]` |
| Each cell block becomes a block of its own | `CN-26-111-A2`, with `A2-S1 [H&E]` cut from it |

Direct smears are not represented as block-independent slides because the schema
does not allow it — `Slide.blockId` is non-nullable, so every slide belongs to a
block. The carrier block is the application's own device for that, not an artifact
of the figure, and no extra block was created to account for the second smear.

The gross description now reads "Received 250 mL of turbid straw-colored pleural
fluid. Centrifuged; one cell block and two smears prepared." Accessioning counts,
gross description, block count, slide count, slide types and the ancillary source
block now all agree, and the material panel asserts the slide types rather than
merely rendering them, so a future drift fails the capture run.

## Which ancillary markers are displayed

**Estrogen receptor alone**, on the cell block (`CN-26-111-A2`), at its terminal
`Distributed` state — the state in which the stained slide has reached the
pathologist, which is what the report comments on.

This is the instructions' "acceptable alternative" rather than the preferred
breast-oriented panel, for two reasons:

1. The seeded catalogue holds ER, PR, HER2 and Ki67 but no GATA3, TRPS1,
   mammaglobin or GCDFP-15. A breast-specific panel would require changing
   `prisma/seed.ts`, which would move the corpus counts already published in the
   verification artifacts.
2. Merging report history into Figure 5 leaves 744 CSS px of page height for three
   panels at 9 pt. A four-row ancillary table needs ~400 px on its own, which would
   push result entry and report history off the page. The merge was explicitly
   preferred in the instructions and the deliverable filenames assume it.

The comment was therefore narrowed to what one marker supports: *"Estrogen receptor
is positive in the malignant cells. This is consistent with, but not specific for, a
breast primary; correlation with the prior clinical history is recommended."* The
claim that immunohistochemistry establishes breast origin is gone, and the
reactivation reason was reworded to match.

## How report history was merged

Report history became **Figure 5C**, and the multilingual figure was renumbered
7 → 6. Figure 5 is now a three-panel figure: ancillary testing, result entry and
authorization, report versioning. To fit three panels at 9 pt, panel B is anchored
on the authorization toolbar so it opens on the sign-out controls and spends its
height on the diagnosis and comment instead of repeating the case header the other
panels already carry. Version 1 remains Final with its pathologist, timestamp and
PDF; version 2 remains an unsigned revision draft; the ancillary timestamp and the
report timestamp agree.

## What multilingual content was added

Figure 6 previously stopped above the reporting fields, showing navigation and
buttons only. Two changes bring the reporting interface into frame:

- the keyboard-layout notice is hidden for the capture (the manuscript does not
  discuss keyboard entry, and the banner cost 60 px of the height needed to reach
  the fields);
- the panels now reach the translated **Final Diagnosis** and **Comment** headings
  with their content visible.

**This reverses an earlier instruction** to leave the free-text fields blank. Blank
fields were chosen so that English prose under an Arabic heading could not be read
as a translation claim, but they also meant the panel showed no report at all. The
fields now show their real content, and the caption states plainly that the
application translates the interface and its structured fields but not narrative
text, so the diagnosis and comment appear in English in both panels. Say the word if
you would rather have the blank version back.

The Arabic panel shows right-to-left flow, mirrored navigation, right-aligned
labels, correctly ordered mixed Arabic/Latin content (`CN-26-111`, `P3242279`,
`Mwangi, Joseph`), the accession and the `مسودة v2` version chip, with no
bidirectional rendering defects. The Kiswahili panel shows `MUHTASARI WA MGONJWA`
in full — tab labels now wrap instead of being clipped.

## Which remaining labels were localized

`OPEN PDF` and `DOWNLOAD PDF` in the patient-summary card now follow the summary's
language (`FUNGUA PDF`, `PAKUA PDF`), joining the title, category line and section
headings localized earlier. The `EN`/`SW` codes are unchanged, since they name the
languages themselves. Everything a patient sees in that card is now in the patient's
language; the interface around it stays in the laboratory's.

**Not done:** the bilingual clinical review of the Kiswahili summary text. That
needs a qualified reviewer, and the reviewer and date should be recorded before
submission. Nothing in the figures or captions claims the translations are validated.

## Supplementary figure checks

- **S2** — caption now describes a JSON-backed template editor, not a no-code one.
- **S3** — the catalogue holds two distinct H&E entries, and neither is legacy:
  `HE` → `H&E Staining` is the routine per-block stain, system-managed and excluded
  from the user-facing category selector; it carries **1078 orders** in the seeded
  corpus, one per block, so removing it would break the routine workflow.
  `HE_LEVELS` → `H&E Levels` is the user-orderable deeper-levels test. The panel
  expands the immunohistochemistry category, so only `H&E Levels` is in frame; the
  remaining categories continue below it.

## Web copies in the repository README

The root README embeds Figures 3, 5 and 6. Those images are not a separate capture
and involve no post-processing: `pnpm figures:compose` renders each one a second time
from the *same* composed layout at a lower device scale factor, so the only difference
from the journal PNG is pixel density.

| Figure | Journal PNG | Web copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 Accessioning | 3344 × 3552 | 1200 × 1275, 95 KB |
| 5 Ancillary, reporting, versioning | 3152 × 3308 | 1200 × 1259, 150 KB |
| 6 Multilingual interfaces | 3344 × 3544 | 1200 × 1272, 188 KB |

They are written to `docs/images/`, which is tracked, unlike `docs/manuscript/`. The
composer refuses to write a web copy over 250 KB, so the tracked set cannot quietly
grow. Checksums are recorded in `docs/images/README.md` and in the checksum table of
`docs/manuscript/figures/README.md`.
